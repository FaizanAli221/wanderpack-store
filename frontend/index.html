Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery, locks, and order guidelines in Pakistan

How do I set the 3-digit TSA lock code on my suitcase? ▼
Default code is 0-0-0. Hold the latch open, scroll the 3 dials to your secret combination, and release the latch.
How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work across Pakistan? ▼
No advance payment needed. Place your order online and hand cash to the courier rider upon delivery at your door.
What are the delivery timelines for Karachi versus other cities? ▼
Karachi express dispatch takes 24-48 hours. Nationwide courier delivery (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, etc.) takes 2 to 4 working days via TCS / Trax.
