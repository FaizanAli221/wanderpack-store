Policies & FAQs

Shipping & Return Policy

Clear, transparent guidelines for stress-free shopping across Pakistan.

🚚 Delivery Timelines

• Karachi Express: 2 to 4 hours delivery available for urgent orders upon phone verification.

• Standard Karachi: 24 to 48 hours delivery.

• Nationwide (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Faisalabad, etc.): 3 to 5 working days via TCS / Trax / Leopards Courier.

📦 Shipping Charges

• FREE Delivery on all orders containing products above Rs. 5,000.

• Flat rate Rs. 250 shipping on orders below Rs. 5,000 anywhere in Pakistan.

🔄 7-Day Exchange & Return Policy

If you receive a defective bag or the size/color doesn't match your expectations, you can request an exchange within 7 calendar days of delivery. Items must be in original condition with tags intact.

💵 Cash on Delivery (COD)

No credit card required. You pay cash to the courier rider right when the package is safely delivered at your doorstep.
