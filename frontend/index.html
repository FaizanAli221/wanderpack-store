Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing orders, COD, luggage locks, and delivery in Pakistan.

How do I set or change the 3-digit TSA lock on my hardshell luggage? ▼
Default factory code is 0-0-0. To change it: slide and hold the release latch in the open direction, turn the dials to your secret 3-digit combination, and release the latch. Your new code is now set! Always remember to write down your code.
How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work across Pakistan? ▼
You do not need to pay in advance. When placing an order, simply select Cash on Delivery. Once our verification team confirms your phone number, your order is dispatched. You hand over cash to the courier rider upon delivery at your home or office.
Can I open and inspect the package before paying the courier rider? ▼
Yes! Under our Open Parcel Guarantee, you can open the flyer to ensure the correct bag model, color, and size arrived before paying. If there is any discrepancy, you can return it immediately with the rider without paying anything.
How fast is delivery in Karachi versus other cities? ▼
For Karachi addresses, we offer 2 to 4 hour express motorbike delivery for urgent orders, or standard next-day delivery. For Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, and other cities, delivery takes 2 to 4 working days via TCS / Trax.
What if the school bag or luggage is damaged during courier transit? ▼
All shipments are 100% insured. If a product arrives damaged, notify us within 48 hours via phone helpline (+92 331 3201912) or email with a photo. We will immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement free of charge.
